# Duelers Guild / The next hand

New concept identity, September 21, 2026. Separate from the original blue preview.

The design treats the Guild as a confident, welcoming tabletop club. Oversized rounded typography, a sculptural gaming still life, a shield mark, and tactile curved panels establish the identity. Real product photography remains factual catalog content.

Palette: aubergine #302039 (primary ink), orange #FF713E (hero and action accent), lilac #E5DCF3 (community and supporting surfaces), ivory #FFFAF2 (reading surface), purple #68468B (links), muted plum #716777 (secondary text).

Type: Bricolage Grotesque 600/800 for headlines and identity; Outfit 400/600 for navigation, forms, and reading. Rounded display headlines are sentence case; no hand-lettering or brush effects. Body text starts at 16px.

Layout: an edge-to-edge orange opening with an asymmetric headline/image split, useful player shortcuts, a compact four-column game collection, then a lilac community section and plum visit section. Interior pages use the same masthead, player navigation, typography and forms, with generous reading surfaces. Tools retain their real behavior and local storage contracts.

Logo: new geometric shield/doorway mark, orange and ivory. Use the primary mark at 48px or larger; the simpler split-diamond favicon stays legible at small sizes. Wordmark uses the display face in two compact lines.

Motion: coordinated opening, restrained card movement and existing pause/reduced-motion controls. No navigation interception.

Preservation: every captured route, catalog record, product photo, public business fact, and local tool is carried forward. Live stock, payments, customer accounts and buylist feeds are still unconnected; the visible preview status remains accurate.

Sources: Google Fonts serves Bricolage Grotesque and Outfit; font files are stored locally. Generated hero artwork is illustrative, not a photo of the store. Existing product images retain their provenance in docs/game-images.json and docs/product-images.json.
